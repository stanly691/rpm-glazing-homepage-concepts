Manufacture-only service

Aluminium glazing manufactured for your installation team

Supply-only fabrication of curtain walling, windows, doors and shopfronts from our Bridgend facility, for general builders, contractors and installation-only companies.

Trade manufacturing

Our manufacturing, your installation

Not every project needs a full design-and-install package. RPM’s manufacture-only service gives general builders, main contractors and installation-only companies access to the same controlled fabrication we use on our own commercial projects.

Send us your drawings, schedules or survey sizes and we will review the requirement, confirm the specification and manufacture to an agreed programme, ready for your own team to install.

We work mainly across South Wales and the South West, and can supply further afield depending on the job.

01

General builders

Commercial-grade aluminium glazing for new-build, extension and refurbishment work, without subcontracting the installation.

02

Installation-only companies

Frames, doors and screens fabricated to your sizes, so your fitters can concentrate on site.

03

Main contractors

A dependable manufacturing partner for glazing packages you prefer to install with your own teams.
